Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Before You Call?

What types of electrical work do you handle?

Common electrical projects include outlets, circuits, lighting, panel work, remodeling electrical work and EV charger circuits. Call to confirm your specific project.

How do I request electrical service?

Call (704) 726-3089 to discuss your needs. An online request form can also be used once the GoHighLevel integration is connected.

What information should I provide when calling?

Share your location, a brief description of the electrical issue or project, and any relevant scheduling considerations.

Do you serve my area?

Ace Electric Service LLC is based in Mooresville, North Carolina. Call to confirm service availability for your address.

When should I contact a professional electrician?

Contact a qualified electrician for repeated breaker trips, damaged wiring, overheating outlets, new circuits or other electrical installation needs.

For immediate danger, keep clear and contact emergency services or your utility as appropriate.
